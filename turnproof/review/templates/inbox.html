Webhook inbox stands in for the airline's claims system

When the handler sends a packet (and when the airline answers), TurnProof POSTs the signed packet to every subscribed endpoint with an HMAC-SHA256 signature over the body. In production the target is the airline's system; in this demo it is this inbox, so the whole loop is visible on one machine.
